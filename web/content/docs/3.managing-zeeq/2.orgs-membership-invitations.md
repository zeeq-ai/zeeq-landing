## Summary

All content in Zeeq is organized into organizations.

Whenever a user signs up, a distinct organization is created.

## Teams

Teams group active members within an organization. A person can belong to several teams at once. Organization owners and admins can manage teams from **Settings → Teams** or choose **Manage teams** on the **Members** page.

### Create a team

1. In **Settings → Teams**, choose **New team**.
2. Enter a team name and an optional description. Team names are unique within an organization, regardless of capitalization.
3. Optionally select initial members by searching for their name or email. Your selections carry across searches.
4. Choose **Create team** to open its roster. You can create an empty team and add members later.

### Manage a roster

The Teams list shows each team's name, description, and active member count. Select a team to open its roster. The roster and picker show each person's profile photo or initials.

- Under **Add members**, search by name or email and select the people to add. The **Add** button shows how many people you selected. The picker only shows eligible people, so invitees appear after they accept and existing team members stay in the roster.
- Choose **Remove from team** beside a person to remove that assignment. They remain in the organization and keep their other team assignments.
- From **Settings → Members**, choose **View teams** on a person's row. Select a team in the panel to open its roster.

Team membership is for grouping. Access to libraries, repositories, and reviews continues to follow the organization's existing access rules. When someone leaves the organization, their team assignments end. If they rejoin, an owner or admin can assign them to teams again.

::solution-features
---
title: Create consistent AI engineering governance
description: Use Zeeq to standardize knowledge, reviews, and telemetry across heterogeneous engineering teams while helping portfolio companies get up to speed on AI delivery practices.
features:
  - icon: i-lucide-git-pull-request-arrow
    title: Portfolio review signals
    description: Track recurring quality risks, review findings, and repository patterns across teams using the same evaluation layer. Operating partners get comparable software quality signals while seeing where portfolio companies need coaching or stronger AI delivery habits.
  - icon: i-lucide-book-marked
    title: Shared operating standards
    description: Publish reusable engineering guidance while preserving company-specific context, constraints, and implementation details. This gives companies a practical starting point for AI engineering best practices without ignoring their local architecture and product realities.
  - icon: i-lucide-gauge
    title: Adoption visibility
    description: See where agents are being used, which knowledge they read, and how that usage relates to shipped code. Leadership can identify teams turning AI adoption into durable delivery outcomes and teams that need more enablement.
  - icon: i-lucide-landmark
    title: Governance without drag
    description: Add observability and guardrails around AI engineering without blocking local teams from choosing their preferred harnesses. The portfolio gains oversight and a repeatable enablement model while engineering teams keep the autonomy they need to move quickly.

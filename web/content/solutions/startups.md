::solution-features
---
title: Build with shared context from day one
description: Use Zeeq to keep agents grounded in your product decisions, engineering standards, and review history as your team scales without adding process bloat.
features:
  - icon: i-lucide-rocket
    title: Fast setup
    description: Connect the agent harnesses your team already uses and start grounding sessions in shared project knowledge. A startup team can improve agent output quickly without pausing product delivery for a heavy platform rollout.
  - icon: i-lucide-brain
    title: Product memory
    description: Preserve architecture decisions, product constraints, and review learnings so agents do not relearn context from scratch. As the team grows, new contributors and agents inherit the same operating context that shaped earlier product decisions.
  - icon: i-lucide-search-check
    title: Review before merge
    description: Catch agent-generated regressions with reviews that cite your own guidance instead of generic best practices. Founders and early engineers can move fast with AI assistance while protecting the codebase they still have to maintain.
  - icon: i-lucide-chart-spline
    title: Signal as you grow
    description: Watch knowledge usage, review trends, and session telemetry before AI tooling becomes hard to reason about. The team gets early evidence of what is working, where quality is slipping, and when to invest in stronger process.

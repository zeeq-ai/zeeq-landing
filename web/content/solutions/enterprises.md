::solution-features
---
title: Standardize agentic engineering across the enterprise
description: Use Zeeq to align AI-assisted delivery across teams, repositories, and micro-services while preserving the local context each group needs to ship responsibly.
features:
  - icon: i-lucide-shield-check
    title: Enterprise standards
    description: Publish engineering standards, security expectations, and review guidance as shared knowledge agents can retrieve during implementation. Teams get consistent guardrails without turning every AI-assisted workflow into a central approval process.
  - icon: i-lucide-network
    title: Disparate micro-services
    description: Ground agents in service-specific architecture, ownership, APIs, and operational constraints across a large distributed estate. Engineers can move between services with more confidence because the relevant context travels into the agent loop.
  - icon: i-lucide-chart-no-axes-combined
    title: Value tracking
    description: Connect token usage, review activity, knowledge reads, and delivery signals so leadership can see where AI investment is producing real engineering outcomes. This helps enterprises move beyond adoption reporting toward measurable value creation.
  - icon: i-lucide-share-2
    title: Cross-org knowledge sharing
    description: Reuse proven guidance across business units while keeping team-specific details scoped to the right audience. Strong patterns spread faster, and local context remains available where it matters most.
---
